# Surface brief: magistick shell (home, apps, updates, directory, help, new hire, admin console)

Mode: Operate. Audience: every BPO employee, Trainee to Super Admin. Job: open apps, read scoped updates, find people and reporting lines, raise tickets; admins manage people, roles, permissions and app access.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The hierarchy and the departments are visible identity, not grey labels. Refuses the grey-SaaS-plus-one-accent admin and the old Glowstick costume.

OWN-WORLD: Jaipur/Bagru hand-block print. Deep indigo shell (top bar, admin rail), pale indigo-tinted ground, white work panels. Twelve department dyes (indigo, madder red, haldi, dhaavda green, kashish brown, lac rose, iron teal, saffron, slate blue, olive, maroon, peacock) each paired with a small geometric "thappa" stamp motif. Inter throughout, tabular numerals. Raised from Metro map (declined): colour-as-wayfinding discipline, a department's dye means that department everywhere and nowhere else.

STORY: Employee lands, sees their name, level, department, manager; their apps; updates for their department or all. Super Admin sees the console and can change any rule live.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Indigo top bar (wordmark, nav, quick links, search, user). A 6px stamp band in the user's department dye under it. Greeting row with level + department + manager chips. App launcher grid left 2/3, announcement panel right 1/3. Updates feed below with For my department / All toggle.

FORM: block-print world, candidate 7 of 7, seed:795c7404b015ce4a. Signature move: department thappa stamps on band, avatars, app tiles, matrix column heads, directory chain.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
